A visual timer for deep focus

Focus Timer is a free, fullscreen countdown timer. Choose a duration and a color, press Start, and the color slowly shrinks from right to left as time passes. When the screen is fully black, your session is done. You can see how much time is left at a glance, without reading numbers.

How to use it

  1. Pick a preset (5 to 60 minutes) or type your own hours, minutes and seconds.
  2. Choose a background color, or make your own with the color picker.
  3. Press Start Timer. The timer opens fullscreen, and in landscape on phones.

Questions

Is Focus Timer free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up and nothing to install.

Can I use it as a Pomodoro timer?

Yes. Pick the 25 minute preset for a focus block and the 5 minute preset for a break.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On Android it goes fullscreen and turns to landscape automatically. On iPhone, rotate your phone sideways for the landscape view.

Does the timer stay accurate if I switch tabs?

Yes. The timer is based on the clock, not on counting ticks, so it stays correct when you pause, switch tabs or lock your screen.

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes. Use the Completion sound switch before you start, or the bell button while the timer runs.
